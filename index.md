# Welcome to Resultron!

**Resultron** is a lightweight .NET library designed to help you manage operation results (Success/Failure), 
error handling, and business rules in a clean, readable, and sustainable way.

### Why Resultron?
* **Avoid Exceptions:** Avoid using `try-catch` blocks for control flow; return functional, explicit results instead.
* **Clean Code:** Minimize complex, nested `if-else` blocks in your controllers and business layers.
* **Flexible Error Handling:** Categorize, group, and match errors effortlessly based on your application's needs.

Get started instantly with our [Getting Started](docs/getting-started.md).
